Empty, loading and error states

What Survivor screens show when there's nothing yet, while data loads, or when something fails
EmptyIdol vault before the draft
No idols yetIdols are hidden on players before the draft. If you draft one, AF Host will DM you when the last pick is in.
EmptyIdol vault after the draft
You didn't find an idolNobody but the holders knows where they are. Watch the scroll for idols being played.
EmptyExile Island before anyone's out
Exile Island is emptyThe first manager voted out lands here and starts earning tokens.
LoadingLeague home
LoadingScroll waiting to unroll
Counting the votes…The scroll opens once every vote is in and idols are resolved.
ErrorScroll failed to load
We couldn't open the scrollYour connection dropped. The results are safe and nothing was revealed.
ClosedVoting closed
Votes are closedTribal Council results are being read. You'll get a notification when the scroll opens.
MissedYou didn't vote
Your vote wasn't castVotes closed at 8:00 PM before you voted, so your vote didn't count this week. Turn on reminders so it doesn't happen again.
SpectatorVoted out, Exile off
You're watching from the sidelinesYour torch is out. You can still read chat, follow the scroll and see the finale.
PausedCommissioner paused the league
The league is pausedThe commissioner paused Survivor events. Lineups and scoring still run. Deadlines move when it resumes.
LockedSettings after the draft
This setting is lockedTribe count and idols lock when the draft starts so nobody can change the game mid-season.
ErrorMoment page turned off
This league is privateThe commissioner turned off public moment pages. Sign in if you're in this league.
