# Draft HQ: broader Phase 4

The selected archive now exposes team components, frozen-roster dynasty/keeper analysis, pick replay, recorded asset transfers and weekly original-team player contribution. Both universal and league-selected Draft HQ use the same permission-scoped archive. No database migration is required.

## Definitions and publication rules

- Roster strength is the optimal legal starter lineup under the scoring rules preserved at draft start. Existing dynasty/keeper players must have an unambiguous frozen roster binding and preserved projection identity. Missing rosters prevent comparable ranks. Starter improvement measures the after-minus-before lineup baseline.
- Opportunity compares the recorded selection's immediate legal-lineup gain with the best remaining gain at that pick. Earlier picks, frozen existing players and all reserved keepers are unavailable. Later selections are not removed early. The UI compares one substitution; it does not simulate later opponents or claim a season outcome.
- Construction is starting-slot coverage. Completely covered teams tie on this component; it is not an independently predictive strategy score. Depth is positive bench value above the league-wide starting-demand replacement proxy. That proxy does not represent current waiver availability.
- ADP discount is an independent market benchmark: recorded overall minus historical ADP. Positive means selected later than ADP. It is not automatically a good football decision. Missing historical ADP remains unavailable.
- Grades require a compatible model observed before the selected draft, with a holdout season earlier than the selected season. Weights are selected on older independent leagues, with every league kept in one split. Minimum independent league counts are 30 training and 15 later-season holdout. Publication requires at least 10% lower held-out mean absolute percentile error than starter strength alone, and error at most 20 percentile points. These thresholds are internal publication requirements, not externally validated standards. The target is original-team finalized starter-contribution percentile, not wins or championship probability.
- Current dynasty market marks are dated and visibly credited separately from draft-date marks and immediate projected points. No trade profit, allocated package price or future-pick outcome is inferred.
- Asset links require recorded player IDs or season/round/original-owner pick identity. Generic pick assets remain ambiguous when multiple same-season drafts could match. Explicit source IDs must agree. Package size and reversals remain visible; transfers are evidence links, not a proven complete causal ownership chain. Reads above 1,000 packages block instead of silently truncating. Future/unresolved assets are paginated.
- Weekly contribution counts recorded points and actual starts on the selecting team only, after the draft, in all-team finalized weeks. Missing rows are partial, not zero. Usage is starts divided by covered final weeks, not games active. Early/late totals divide observed weeks in half. Weekly replacement VOR requires replacement eligibility evidence and is not inferred from aggregate results.

## Evidence and limits

The production read-only calibration rehearsal on 2026-10-04 examined **zero eligible completed historical v2 cohorts** and wrote no model. Letter grades therefore remain unavailable. Historical current projections cannot reconstruct missing pre-draft projections. Imported aggregate result observations remain separately labeled; they cannot supply individual weekly usage or a calibrated training cohort.

Replay comparisons require complete preserved component scoring, eligible pool evidence and supported format/context. Snake/linear standard/startup drafts use recorded order; supported rookie/veteran pools require dated archived membership and auctions require recorded award budgets plus a user-assumed price. Recorded picks remain replayable even when comparisons are unavailable. Missing pick times never acquire a guessed clock.

Local deterministic explanations and links to current lineup, waivers, trades and preparation are included. The approved Chimmy integration explains selected draft facts using the existing AI chat providers with an explicit allowlist and authenticated league access.

## Operations

Run `node --env-file=.env.production --conditions=react-server --import tsx scripts/recompute-draft-calibration.ts` for the read-only rehearsal. It prints aggregate counts only. Apply mode additionally requires `--apply --production` against the verified production database identity. No remote provider requests run in this job. More than the candidate limit, failed source reads, incomplete final-week coverage or failed validation block publication. Re-run after sufficient completed seasons with original v2 snapshots accumulate. Model observations are append-only and never backdated. No automatic provider ingestion or new scheduler is introduced.

The release must pass existing protected-branch CI and normal merge requirements before Railway deployment. Verify the served SHA and database health after deployment. Roll back the application commit if archive access, render stability or latency regresses. There are no schema changes to reverse; newly stored calibration observations can remain unused by the prior application.

Responsive fixture validation covers 320, 390, 768 and 1440 CSS-pixel widths, bounded table scrolling, replay selection/search/scenarios, and question-mark hover/click/Escape behavior. This is Chromium fixture testing, not physical iOS/Android testing or authenticated live-league acceptance testing. English and Spanish copy are present.

## Research basis

[FantasyPros Draft Analyzer](https://draftwizard.fantasypros.com/football/draft-analyzer/) motivates transparent component comparisons. [FantasyPros value-based drafting definitions](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005868747-What-is-value-based-drafting-What-do-player-draft-values-mean-VORP-VONA-VOLS-VBD) distinguish replacement-value baselines from market draft position. Neither source validates AllFantasy's model weights. [Sleeper's official API](https://docs.sleeper.com/) supplies documented original/previous/current pick-owner fields and transaction package identities. [Scikit-learn cross-validation guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html) supports group/time separation to avoid leakage; [calibration guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html) reinforces held-out evaluation before probability claims. The current model deliberately publishes no win probability.

### Approved Chimmy and remaining evidence support (October 5, 2026)
The owner approved the new Chimmy connection. Draft HQ opens the selected league's Chimmy composer with an exact archive key; the user sends the question. `get_draft_analysis` takes no league ID and uses the authenticated selected league's authorized archive reader. A bounded allowlist shares recorded picks, ADP provenance, timing, team baselines, original-team results, available grades and recorded transfers with the existing AI chat providers. Raw snapshots, actor IDs, private correction notes and raw transaction metadata are excluded. Pick facts paginate 40 at a time. Untrusted labels are data, and the response explicitly prohibits invented historical projections, odds and grades.

Auction awards now preserve the winner's actual budget before the award, unfilled draft slots, minimum bid and award timestamp in the same transaction as the pick. Replay requires this evidence for every non-keeper award, complete original projections and roster coverage. A comparison requires a user-supplied hypothetical price with reserves for all remaining slots. This is a local affordability and lineup scenario; rival bidding, sale price and later reactions are unknown. Auction opportunity components and calibrated grades remain unavailable. Earlier auctions without this evidence are not backfilled from current budgets. Historical auction benchmark lookup also uses the original start-snapshot budget; later commissioner edits cannot change its reference scale. Salary-cap contract/development formats remain outside this comparison.

Future NFL rookie/veteran pool snapshots freeze membership within the preserved projection universe using only verified Sleeper ID mappings and the dated, same-season compact years-experience cache. Cache payloads now carry observation time and the authoritative Sleeper NFL state season; absent season evidence is never replaced with a calendar-year guess. The start transaction performs a database read only. Missing/expired/undated metadata or even one unclassified projected player blocks restricted comparisons. Existing players remain available to the selecting team's baseline but unavailable as candidates. Rookie/development eligibility is never inferred from today's names or years of experience for an older draft. Devy/C2C/dispersal and unsupported sports remain outside this comparison.

Calibration rehearsal accepts `--from-season=2023 --through-season=2025 --league-type=redraft --purpose=standard --team-count=12` (each optional), bound to original archived context. Select whole format cohorts across older and holdout seasons; avoid truncation. Each format remains independent, and failed reads or processing bounds prevent publishing partial weights. Validation requirements are unchanged. A zero eligible corpus means grades remain data-dependent, not an implementation or approval issue.

Research: [Sleeper auction budgets and nominations](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/4056268-do-you-support-auction-drafts) supports keeping budgets and bid scenarios distinct from snake order/ADP. Browser acceptance remains unverified if the authenticated browser helper fails; responsive fixtures do not substitute for physical iOS/Android testing.


### Weekly imported evidence and integrity (October 6, 2026)
Commissioner refreshes now append `draft-results-v2` observations containing bounded player/team identities, expected post-draft scored weeks, original-team weekly points, starter usage and explicit held/not-held evidence. Provider observations remain provisional; they cannot train finalized-result calibration or supply weekly replacement value. The reader validates source, date, inventory and rows, ties identities to the selected archive, recomputes totals and strips unlisted metadata. Weekly evidence uses a separate versioned index; each refresh atomically writes a compatible v1 aggregate observation under the existing key, so the deployed reader and a rollback retain totals. The new reader prefers weekly evidence and falls back to older aggregates. Refresh a supported Sleeper archive to obtain weekly detail. No migration, background fetch or scheduler is introduced.

Duplicate player-week rows, conflicting ownership, duplicate starters, invalid starter flags and incomplete inventories prevent complete rankings. An absent score stays unavailable; a zero after departure requires a complete weekly roster proving absence. The UI distinguishes departure from bench usage, shows observation dates and provisional week coverage, and hides totals/starts when no rows exist. English and Spanish help explain these distinctions.

The existing approved Chimmy evidence payload is unchanged. A proposed separate extension would send only the selected draft's bounded per-player weekly rows (week, original-team points, starter/held flags), coverage counts, provisional status and observation date to the existing AI providers. Automatic approval review rejected that outbound expansion as outside the earlier approval; it requires specific owner approval before implementation. The page does not send these new weekly records to Chimmy.

Production verification on October 6 appended weekly/compatible aggregate observations for the existing two-source bounded refresh. Both source reads succeeded across six scored weeks. The permission-checked evidence-only probe validated 1,044 original-team player-week records across 348 drafted players: one comparable provisional archive and one partial archive. The full page-loader CLI remains blocked by the shared local React 18 runtime; `--weekly-only` avoids that page runtime while retaining actual membership checks and strict evidence validation. Live authenticated browser acceptance also remains blocked by the browser helper ACL startup error. These limitations do not convert provisional results into final results or empirical training cohorts.
