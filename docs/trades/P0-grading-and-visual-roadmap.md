# Trade grading P0 and visual roadmap

## Shipped policy

The shared letter measures the sum of quoted market values plus supported league scoring adjustments. Roster utility remains a separate measurement. Any unpriced asset withholds the grade; nonfinite and negative asset prices now also withhold it.

Uneven packages of three or more assets with explicit asset kinds show the net change in player roster slots. Picks and FAAB occupy no player slots. An A/B package is a review recommendation until a manager checks the outgoing starters and any required drops. We do not apply a blanket star premium or volume discount without a measured replacement cost. Older receipts without asset kinds cannot establish this check.

Evidence labels describe observed coverage and source dates, never accuracy probabilities. Quotes older than seven days are flagged as stale under an operational rule. Formula values, unknown sources, missing dates, and other gaps are named. The selectable ±5/10/20% sensitivity scenario changes outgoing and incoming totals in opposite directions; it is not a confidence interval or independent source consensus.

## Benchmark boundaries

The regression benchmark covers 1-for-1, 2-for-1, 4-for-1, future picks, mirror symmetry, bad prices, and unchanged shared grades when team direction differs. Controlled values are explicitly synthetic. The DK Metcalf for a 2027 second case reproduces an inspected historical price-parity regression (1766 vs 1584). Passing this suite proves policy consistency, not empirical prediction accuracy.

Remaining grading research: replay a representative sample of real completed trades using the values and rosters available at trade time; measure usable starter production, replacements and roster drops. Report performance by format, package size, and time horizon. Tune any consolidation adjustment only after that study. Market agreement and realized production answer different questions and need separate metrics.

## Next: visual trade impact

Before/after bars: projected usable starting lineup production, positional depth, roster slots and market value. A change in projection must name the week/season and settings. The generic analyzer can show asset-value balance and sensitivity; team lineup effects require team context.

Player cards: value history already has an append-only PlayerValueSnapshot reader. Use one source and one format per series. Add week/season controls only for available capture periods. Show gaps and earliest recorded date. Actual fantasy points belong on a separate labeled series from market value and projections.

Desktop/tablet: side-by-side team impacts with tap/click tooltips. Phones: stacked cards with the same units, accessible summary/table, and full-width chart. Color must not be the only indicator.

## Next: Impact now

Preserve the original saved evaluation and its capture date. The existing frozen-completed-grade path already carries today's market regrade. A new button should present original price grade, current price grade, realized production since the trade, and fit with today's roster/goals as separate measures. Name any players subsequently moved and any missing scoring weeks. Never call a later first-observed price the price on the original trade date.

## Next: personality

Accepted trade: brief first-party Chimmy reaction or optional licensed GIF. Team grades: labeled emoji badges and short deterministic captions, such as “Deal sealed 🤝”, “Championship swing 🚀”, “Future loading 🌱”, and “Needs a closer look 🔍”. Use “Championship swing” only when measured lineup impact supports it.

Keep reactions optional, respect reduced motion, and allow league administrators to disable them on shared feeds. Celebrate a completed deal without publicly shaming a manager. Evidence limits must be visible before decorative reactions.
