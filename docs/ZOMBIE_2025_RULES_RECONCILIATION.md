# Zombie 2025 rules reconciliation

## Scope and decisions

Reviewed 2025 Beta Zombie League Rules (20 pages), Zombie league weekly update (2 pages), and Zombie Universe Stats Spreadsheet (6 pages) against staging source `2ebf7f6d3f6f7a619488fffd5882f91d83840e5c`. The owner selected the **2025 PDF rules as the target for weekly winnings and Whisperer trade eligibility**. The PDF is an NFL Beta reference, not a specification for NBA, NHL or MLB. Do not assume its dates, sample usernames, buy-in amounts, 20-team league size, or sports thresholds are universal product defaults. Other differences below are 2025 comparison targets, subject to current league configuration and owner policy.

Status key: **Difference** = conflicting behavior found in the named active path; **Partial** = capability exists but full parity is not established; **Verify** = insufficient evidence to call complete; **Implemented path** = code exists, not a claim of production or live-manager acceptance. This is a source review, not a completed integration test or legal/payment approval.

## Key acceptance scenarios before release

1. For each Survivor opponent role, start with nonzero accumulated winnings, resolve a final matchup, and compare exact transfers with pp. 7–9. Test a 7.21 Zombie-v-Zombie margin (take $8 up to the available balance), ties, zero balance and repeated jobs.
2. Use a multi-week fixture with both infection and later stat correction; verify balances, role, items, posted reports and downstream dependencies. Conflicting external payout records must require explicit review.
3. Survivor bashes Whisperer by exactly 60: assert spare and replacement choices, Wednesday 9 p.m. ET default, remaining ambush transfer and concurrent/retry safety. Exercise Zombie mauling Whisperer with and without two serums.
4. Check each NFL score-band boundary 99.99, 100, 104.99, 105, 109.99, 110, 114.99, 115, 119.99 and 120; apply two weapons or bomb once and test ownership/trade/steal rules.
5. Register multiple serums after one starter begins and before the last begins; enforce per-roster time using authoritative game starts. Verify Zombie vs Survivor/Whisperer opponent eligibility and no same-week revive.
6. Ambush multiple untouched matchups after another team's game starts; prevent changes to affected locked teams and duplicate participants. Assert per-season cap and atomic rollback.
7. Reproduce the weekly report and universe spreadsheet with two tiers, 16+ distinct managers, missing scores, early season completion, live corrections, secrecy, and exact once-only pinned publication.
8. Complete an authenticated manager/commissioner rehearsal and real scoring window for each sport with provider data, then verify actual scheduler, logs and failure notifications.
