## 1. Positioning foundation

**Category (customer-facing):** your fantasy command center — one home for every league you play.
*(Keep "operating system," "Decision OS," "canonical," etc. internal — never on the page.)*

**Positioning statement (internal north star):**
> For serious fantasy managers and commissioners who play across multiple platforms, AllFantasy is the one place that brings every league together — so you always know what needs your attention, who to start, and where to go. Unlike ESPN, Yahoo, or Sleeper, which only show you their own leagues, AllFantasy sits above all of them.

**Who it's for:** (1) multi-league managers drowning in tabs, (2) commissioners who run the chaos for everyone else.

**The wedge, in one breath:** the incumbents are walled gardens; a neutral command center above all of them is the thing they'll never build.

**Voice principles:**
- Lead with the *benefit and the feeling*, never the technology.
- Confident and plain — no jargon, no hype-words you can't back up.
- **Honesty is a feature.** "Real numbers or nothing" is a true, rare claim — use it.
- Respect the scope: "see and decide," never "manage everything from here."
- Skill-based, no gambling — keep the posture clean (a quiet "no DFS, no wagering" builds trust with the right audience).

## 6. Get your time back (the time-saving angle)

*The most resonant angle you have — because it's not about fantasy, it's about life. Use it prominently, near the top and again by the commissioner section.*

**Manager band:**
> ### Spend less time managing. More time living.
> How many hours a week do your fantasy leagues really take? Between checking five apps, reading waiver reports, and second-guessing your lineups, it adds up fast. AllFantasy pulls it all into one place and tells you exactly what needs doing — so you get your Sundays back for family, friends, and actually enjoying the games.

**Alt hook (question-led — great as a section header or ad):**
> Why bounce between five sites to check your teams — when you can open AllFantasy and know exactly what your team needs, right now?

**Commissioner band:**
> ### Run a great league without it running your week.
> Being commissioner is a part-time job you didn't apply for — chasing lineups, settling disputes, posting recaps, keeping everyone engaged. AllFantasy handles the busywork and flags only what actually needs you, so your league runs smoothly and your week stays yours.

**⚠ On the "cut it in half / save 50%" claim — read before publishing:**
Your instinct that time-saved is the emotional core is exactly right. But a hard number like "cut your time in half" is a specific claim, and your single most valuable position is *"we never show you a made-up number."* An unmeasured "50%" quietly contradicts that trust — the first skeptic who asks "based on what?" costs more than the line gains. Two honest ways to keep the punch:
- **Now (aspirational, no number):** "cut the busywork," "get your Sundays back," "less time managing, more time living." Same payoff, nothing to disprove.
- **Soon (earned, with data):** measure it in your beta — ask users their before/after weekly hours (it's already in your metrics list). The moment you have real numbers, *"beta managers cut their weekly fantasy admin by X%"* becomes your strongest line **because** you don't fake numbers. That's the version worth waiting for.
